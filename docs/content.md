## 5. Attribution notes

What the in-app "About & attributions" screen must show, per licence type. The `attribution` column above is the string; the rules below say what goes around it.

**CC BY 3.0 / 4.0** (`jung-naiv-drogen`, `terra-x-friedlaender`, `terra-x-klimafaktoren`, `terra-x-so-trinken-baeume`, `sprite-fright`, `cosmos-laundromat` per IA, `tears-of-steel`, `terra-xplore-aberglaube`, `terra-x-tutanchamun`, `terra-x-reblausplage`, `bfs-strahlenschutz`, `boell-moor-erklaert`, `dlr-rosetta`, `elephants-dream`, `sintel`)

- Show: clip title, the attribution string exactly as in the table, the licence name with its version, and a link to the licence URL from the Licence column.
- State the modification: "Excerpt (in–out), trimmed; subtitles and glosses added by Lingo." CC BY requires that changes are indicated; ZDF's Nutzungsbedingungen say it outright (name creators, document modifications, no distortion, show CC BY 4.0 icon + link).
- ZDF rows: the credit line is the multi-name string in the table; do not shorten it to "ZDF".
- Blender rows: the sharing pages ask for the *entire credits scroll* if you "redistribute or screen or broadcast the movie itself"; a 4-minute excerpt with an attribution card is the "all other cases" path (© Blender Foundation | durian.blender.org). Convention recorded here, to be confirmed by the owner: Lingo shows excerpts with an attribution card, so the short attribution string applies. If a full film is ever played in-app, the credits scroll must stay in.
- Version drift (Elephants Dream 2.5 vs 3.0 US; Cosmos Laundromat BY-SA 3.0 tag vs IA BY 4.0): all variants are attribution-only or attribution+ShareAlike; the app already shows attribution, so this is a bookkeeping note, not a blocker. Record the exact licence per clip in the clip's DB row.

**CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0** (`openhpi-vandalismus`, `terra-x-becker-interview`, `wmde-glam-tour`, `valkaama`; treat `cosmos-laundromat` as BY-SA too because of the Commons tag)

- Everything above, plus the ShareAlike consequence: subtitles/glosses derived from the clip are arguably adaptations; keep derived subtitle files under the same BY-SA licence to stay clean. Concretely, the derived VTT and gloss files for these clips carry CC BY-SA (same version as the source); put a `NOTE` line with the licence and attribution at the top of each VTT and in the manifest.
- Do not mix BY-SA cue files into a bundle that is labelled CC BY.

**Public domain, US (Prelinger / Internet Archive)** (`shy-guy-1947`, `duck-and-cover`, `what-to-do-on-a-date-1950`, `dating-dos-and-donts-1949`, `a-date-with-your-family-1950`)

- Caveat, in the spike's words: Public-domain rows (E7–E11) rest on US public-domain status (non-renewal / US government work). Germany applies life+70 without a rule of shorter term for US works, so an EU launch should treat them as "US-PD" and prefer the CC BY rows (E1–E6, E12) for anything shown in Germany. E10 (US federal commission) and E6 (VOA, US government) are the safest of that set.

**Public domain, VOA** (`voa-lets-learn-english-01`)

## 6. Level and dialogue risk

- German is B1+ across the board. Of 13 passing German rows, only 5 are real spoken interaction (`jung-naiv-drogen`, `openhpi-vandalismus`, `terra-x-becker-interview`, `terra-x-friedlaender`, `wmde-glam-tour`); the other 8 are narrated explainers. Narration is clean for Transcribe but weaker for "learn how people talk". Mitigation the spike proposes: build the German demo on `jung-naiv-drogen` + `openhpi-vandalismus` and use Terra X for variety.
- No German equivalent of VOA "Let's Learn English" (scripted A1–A2 dialogue) exists under a free licence. The closest is `terra-x-so-trinken-baeume` at A2–B1 (approx.), and it is narration.
- English covers A1 (VOA) through B2 with real dialogue at every band, so English-as-target has no gap.
- Timecodes for `jung-naiv-drogen`, `openhpi-vandalismus`, `sprite-fright`, `tears-of-steel`, `elephants-dream`, `cosmos-laundromat`, `sintel`, `shy-guy-1947`, `what-to-do-on-a-date-1950`, `dating-dos-and-donts-1949` are proposals from synopses and subtitle files, not from a viewing; whoever ingests confirms in/out while trimming.
- Editorial: `terra-x-friedlaender` is a Holocaust survivor's testimony (Theresienstadt); glosses and quiz items must be handled with care. `jung-naiv-drogen` is about drug policy (adult, not graphic). `cosmos-laundromat` is rated 13+.

**Open question for the human (not decided here):** the plan allows a fallback of English as the target language with German as the native language for the first release. Taking it gives the demo scripted A1 dialogue, printed transcripts and clean audio on day one, at the cost of the "learn German from German TV" story judges may expect. Staying German-first keeps the story but means the lowest honest level on the menu is A2–B1 (approx.) narration, and the A1–A2 learner has nothing to watch.

## 7. Sourcing notes

Condensed from the spike's friction list. These concern content sources, not Amazon/AWS tools, so they are recorded here rather than in docs/friction.

- blender.org hosts return 403 to fetch tools, and studio.blender.org pages are JS-rendered with no licence text in the HTML. Cite the server-rendered sharing pages (durian.blender.org/sharing, mango.blender.org/sharing) or the archive.org item, never studio.blender.org.
- zdf.de Terra X CC links redirect (308) to schule.zdf.de, and the landing page only says "unter Creative Commons-Lizenz (OER)" without the type. Use the per-clip schule.zdf.de page (it shows "CC BY 4.0", the credit line and a download button) as the source of truth for each Terra X row.
- Commons category pages list 200 files without durations. Use the MediaWiki API (`prop=videoinfo`, `viprop=size|url|extmetadata`, filter 170–600 s) plus a TimedText prefix search for subtitle languages; the spike's script belongs in packages/pipeline as a sourcing helper.
- Python `urllib` on this Mac fails with SSL CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED. Shell out to curl, or `pip install certifi`.
- Internet Archive item pages often hide the rights statement, and some Prelinger items have no licence field at all. Read `https://archive.org/metadata/<id>` (`licenseurl`, `runtime`, `files` incl. .srt/.vtt) and make a present `licenseurl` a hard gate in the ingest script.
- media.ccc.de states the licence only inside the video (outro/intro or container metadata), never on the talk page. If a CCC talk is wanted, ingest one, read the outro frame or the container metadata, and record the quote here before use.
- VOA numeric article URLs can 301 to the wrong site; get canonical lesson links from the index page https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/5644.html.
- valkaama.com's download page is empty (legacy PHP/Flash). Use https://archive.org/details/valkaama for MP4/MKV and the six .srt files; confirm the spoken language by listening before ingest.
- In the sandbox shell `grep` is aliased to ugrep and rejects long regexes; use Python `re` for wide-window matching.

## 8. Ingest checklist (one clip)

- Pick the row; copy Slug, Segment, Licence and Attribution string from the table above.
- Verify the licence is still there: open the Source URL (Commons file page, schule.zdf.de clip page, or `https://archive.org/metadata/<id>` → `licenseurl`). If it is gone or changed, stop and update this file first.
- Download with the Direct file URL: `curl -L -o <slug>.src.<ext> "<direct file URL>"` (VOA: use the on-page download menu, 1080p).
- Check the source: `ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration:stream=codec_name,width,height -of default=nw=1 <slug>.src.<ext>` and compare with the Duration column.
- Cut with the in/out timecodes: `ffmpeg -ss <in> -to <out> -i <slug>.src.<ext> -c copy <slug>.mp4`. `-c copy` snaps to the nearest keyframe, so the in-point can drift by a few seconds; if the drift cuts a word, drop `-c copy` and re-encode (`-c:v libx264 -c:a aac`). Watch the first and last 10 s and adjust in/out; write the final timecodes back into the table.
- For rows marked "confirm by listening/viewing", also confirm the segment is the dialogue-dense part described in the Why column.
- Fill the Clip row in `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma` terms: `slug`, `title`, `sourceLang`, `durationS` (from ffprobe of the cut), `license` (the short label from the Licence column, e.g. `CC BY 4.0`, `CC BY-SA 4.0`, `Public domain (US)`), `attribution` (the Attribution string, character for character).
- BY-SA rows: add the licence and attribution as a `NOTE` at the top of every derived VTT and in the manifest (see Attribution notes).
- Upload the cut to S3, then run `pnpm pipeline prepare --clip <slug> --source <s3uri> --lang <de|en>` (add `--native <codes>` to override the default native-language list).
- If an existing transcript is listed in the table, keep it next to the pipeline output and diff it against the Transcribe result before publishing.
- Set `status` from `draft` to published only after the attributions screen shows the row.
